Our new preprint, led by Maria A. R. A. Castro, examines how hazard diversity and compound conditions shape recorded disaster impacts across Brazilian municipalities. Using 76,191 records from the Brazilian Digital Atlas of Disasters from 1991 to 2025, the study finds that compound conditions occurred in 35.8% of classified events and accounted for 36.8% of recorded economic losses. The work also introduces PRISMA Brazil, an open platform for municipal disaster records, socio-environmental indicators, and multi-hazard analysis.
