Published in *Sustainability*, this study develops a modular approach for sizing bioretention systems under changing rainfall and urbanization conditions. It evaluates how design requirements change as runoff generation and climatic forcing depart from historical assumptions. The work supports drainage designs that can be adjusted as future conditions and local constraints become clearer.
