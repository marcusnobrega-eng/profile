This paper evaluates a serious game designed to communicate dam-break flooding and emergency decisions. The activity connects simulated flood behavior with the choices made by people exposed to the hazard. The study examines how interactive tools can support risk awareness when conventional maps or technical reports are difficult for non-specialists to interpret.
