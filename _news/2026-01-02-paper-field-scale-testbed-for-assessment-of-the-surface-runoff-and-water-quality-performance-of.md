This paper describes a field-scale test bed for measuring the hydrologic and water-quality performance of permeable pavements. The experimental system compares surface types under controlled inflow and observed environmental conditions. The resulting measurements provide a basis for evaluating runoff reduction, infiltration, and pollutant treatment while supporting calibration of pavement models.
