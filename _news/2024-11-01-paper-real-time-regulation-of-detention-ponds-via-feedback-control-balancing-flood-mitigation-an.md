This study develops feedback-control strategies for operating detention ponds in real time. It evaluates how dynamic releases alter the trade-off between retaining water for flood mitigation and managing water-quality objectives. The results clarify when controlled operation can improve the use of existing storage compared with a fixed outlet configuration.
