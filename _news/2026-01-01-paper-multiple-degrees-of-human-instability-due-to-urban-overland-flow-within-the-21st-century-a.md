This study evaluates human instability under urban overland flow using combinations of water depth and velocity. It examines how the spatial distribution of pedestrian hazard changes under present and future rainfall conditions in a Brazilian watershed. The work shifts flood assessment from water levels alone toward the physical conditions that affect people moving through flooded areas.
