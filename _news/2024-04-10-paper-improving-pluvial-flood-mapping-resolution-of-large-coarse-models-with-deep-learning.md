Published in *Hydrological Sciences Journal*, this study uses deep learning to recover finer spatial detail from coarse pluvial-flood simulations. It tests whether information from terrain and coarse model outputs can improve local flood mapping without running every simulation at the finest resolution. The work addresses the computational gap between regional flood analysis and the street-scale information needed to evaluate exposure.
