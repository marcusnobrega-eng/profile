  for urban drainage and dam-break applications'

This study evaluates the spatial and temporal behavior of two-dimensional local-inertial hydrodynamic models in urban drainage and dam-break applications. It examines how resolution and numerical settings affect predicted flood depths, velocities, and propagation. The comparison helps define when a computationally efficient formulation preserves the flood characteristics needed for each application.
