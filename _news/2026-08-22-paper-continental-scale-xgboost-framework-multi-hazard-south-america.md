---

Published in *Sustainable Cities and Society*, this paper develops a consistent XGBoost framework for mapping flood, drought, and wildfire susceptibility together with socioeconomic exposure across South America. The analysis uses the same methodological basis for every municipality, allowing hazards and vulnerability to be compared without changes in the underlying procedure. The resulting maps identify where multiple hazards coincide with populations and infrastructure that may be more exposed.
