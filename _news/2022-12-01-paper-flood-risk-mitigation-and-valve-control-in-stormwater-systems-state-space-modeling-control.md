This paper develops state-space representations and control algorithms for regulating valves in stormwater systems. Through case studies, it examines how controlled storage and discharge can reduce flood risk while respecting operational limits. The work provides a mathematical basis for moving detention infrastructure from passive operation toward adaptive control.
