This paper develops open-source software for steady and unsteady one-dimensional hydrodynamic simulation and compares different hydraulic conceptualizations through case studies. It examines how assumptions about flow representation affect computed water levels and routing behavior. The work makes the governing choices explicit so users can select a formulation suited to the process and scale being studied.
