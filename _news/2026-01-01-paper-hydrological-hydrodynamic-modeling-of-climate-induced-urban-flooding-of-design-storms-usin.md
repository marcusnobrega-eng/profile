This paper uses HydroPol2D to propagate climate-adjusted design storms through an urban hydrologic and hydrodynamic model of Sao Carlos, Brazil. It evaluates how altered rainfall affects runoff generation and local flooding, rather than stopping at changes in rainfall intensity. The study connects climate scenarios directly with flood variables used in urban drainage planning.
