This perspective examines how nature-based stormwater systems can be combined with real-time control. It discusses the sensing, forecasting, modeling, and operational requirements of this integration, together with the uncertainties that limit implementation. Connecting passive storage with adaptive operation may improve performance without requiring the same expansion in physical infrastructure.
