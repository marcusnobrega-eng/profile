This paper develops a digital twin for real-time probabilistic forecasting of permeable-pavement performance. The framework combines a process-based model with monitoring data and uncertainty analysis to update predictions as observations become available. It supports evaluation of infiltration, storage, and maintenance needs while acknowledging that pavement condition and future forcing are uncertain.
