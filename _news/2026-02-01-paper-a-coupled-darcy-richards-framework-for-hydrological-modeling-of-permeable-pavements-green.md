This paper develops DRAIN-LID, a coupled Darcy-Richards framework for simulating unsaturated flow through permeable pavements, green roofs, and bioretention systems. The common formulation represents layered porous media while retaining the boundary conditions of each intervention. The open-source model supports consistent comparison of infiltration-based systems that are often analyzed with separate tools.
