title: 'Paper: Generalizing rapid flood predictions to unseen urban catchments with
  conditional generative adversarial networks'
collection: news
date: '2023-03-01'
type: Paper
link: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jhydrol.2023.129276
link_label: DOI
---

This study tests whether a conditional generative adversarial network can produce rapid flood maps for urban catchments that were not included in model training. It addresses a central limitation of data-driven flood emulators: performance outside the locations represented in the training data. The results inform how machine-learning models may support faster mapping across many urban basins.
