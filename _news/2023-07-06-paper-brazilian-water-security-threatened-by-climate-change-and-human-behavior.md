This study examines how climate variability and human water use jointly affect water security across Brazil. It combines hydrologic modeling with observations to separate environmental forcing from management-related pressures. The analysis shows why water availability cannot be interpreted from climate trends alone, particularly where withdrawals, storage, and land-use change alter the hydrologic response.
