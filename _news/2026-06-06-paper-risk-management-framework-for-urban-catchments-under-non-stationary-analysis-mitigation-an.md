Published in *Urban Water Journal*, this paper develops a risk-management framework for evaluating urban catchments under nonstationary rainfall and land-use conditions. It compares mitigation and adaptation scenarios in a Brazilian case study by propagating changing drivers into flood outcomes. The framework helps distinguish measures that address current deficiencies from those needed to preserve performance under future conditions.
