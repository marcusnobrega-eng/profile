Published in *Water Resources Management*, this paper introduces X-WHAT, an open-source spreadsheet model for simulating and optimizing looped water-distribution networks. It brings hydraulic calculations and design decisions into an accessible Excel workflow that can be inspected and modified by users. The study evaluates the model through network applications and connects hydraulic performance with pipe-design choices.
