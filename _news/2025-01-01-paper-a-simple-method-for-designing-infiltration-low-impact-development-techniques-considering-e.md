This paper presents a design method for infiltration-based low-impact development systems under changing urbanization and rainfall conditions. It links projected runoff changes to the storage and infiltration capacity required from the intervention. The method helps engineers examine whether a design based on present conditions will retain its intended hydrologic performance.
