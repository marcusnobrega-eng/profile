This paper introduces HydroPol2D, an open-source distributed model that couples rainfall, infiltration, runoff, surface flow, shallow groundwater, drainage infrastructure, and pollutant transport on a raster grid. Case studies evaluate the model under limited observational data. The work provides a transparent platform for studying floods and water quality where conventional monitoring networks are sparse.
