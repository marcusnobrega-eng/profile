This study compares bioretention configurations designed to reduce runoff and support stormwater harvesting under Brazilian conditions. It examines how design choices alter storage, infiltration, and water availability. The analysis helps distinguish configurations intended mainly for peak-flow mitigation from those that also retain water for later use.
