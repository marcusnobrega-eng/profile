Published in *Journal of Hydroinformatics*, this paper asks what is required to scale digital twins of nature-based solutions from individual sites to larger stormwater and transboundary water systems. It discusses the links among monitoring, process-based models, data exchange, and operational decisions. The study identifies technical and institutional constraints that must be addressed before digital twins can support water management across locations and jurisdictions.
