This preprint develops national-scale empirical Huff curves for Brazil from 290,164 sub-daily storm events. It examines how the timing of rainfall within a storm varies across locations, durations, and event characteristics. The resulting distributions provide locally derived inputs for design-storm construction and help test the flood consequences of using temporal patterns developed for other regions.
