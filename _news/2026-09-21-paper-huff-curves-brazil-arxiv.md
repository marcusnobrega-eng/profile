Our preprint, "Time Distribution of Heavy Rainfall in Brazil: Empirical Huff Curves from 290,164 Sub-Daily Storm Events," by Marcus Nobrega Gomes Junior, André Simões Ballarin, and Paulo Tarso Sanches de Oliveira, is now available on *arXiv*. The study develops the first national-scale empirical Huff curves for Brazil using 290,164 storm events. [Read the preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25315).
