---

This paper develops an open-source flood forecasting system that links a digital twin with hydrologic and hydrodynamic simulation. The framework is designed for regions where observations and operational infrastructure are limited. It shows how models, incoming data, and forecast updates can be organized into a reproducible workflow for anticipating flood extent and timing.
