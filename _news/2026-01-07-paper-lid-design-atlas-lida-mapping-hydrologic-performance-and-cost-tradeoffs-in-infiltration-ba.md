This paper introduces the LID-Design Atlas, a framework for mapping the hydrologic performance and cost of infiltration-based stormwater systems across design conditions. It allows users to compare runoff control with the space and investment required by each alternative. The atlas makes trade-offs visible before a detailed site design is selected.
