This paper develops a model and software workflow for designing low-impact development systems while preserving spatial differences in runoff generation. It examines how the location and contributing area of an intervention affect its required capacity. The approach moves beyond uniform catchment assumptions and connects local runoff sources with distributed stormwater design.
