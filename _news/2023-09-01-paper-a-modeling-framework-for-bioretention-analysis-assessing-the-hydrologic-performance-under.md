  performance under system uncertainty'

This paper develops a modeling framework for evaluating bioretention performance under uncertainty in design parameters and environmental conditions. It tracks how uncertainty propagates into runoff reduction, storage, and infiltration responses. The study helps identify which parameters require better measurements before a bioretention design can be evaluated with confidence.
