Published in *Revista DAE*, this paper evaluates statistical, graphical, and nonparametric criteria for fitting intensity-duration-frequency curves in Sao Carlos, Brazil. It also translates the fitted relationships into design charts for hydraulic structures. The study shows why the choice and evaluation of an IDF equation matter when rainfall estimates are used in engineering design.
