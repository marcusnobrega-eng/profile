This study evaluates the socioeconomic effects of the Agreste Water Supply System in Pernambuco, Brazil. It connects changes in water access with indicators of local development and household conditions. The analysis helps assess large water-supply investments through the benefits experienced by the municipalities and populations they are intended to serve.
