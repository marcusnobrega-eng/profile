  durations on flood hazard modeling'

Published in *Natural Hazards*, this study examines how rainfall timing and storm duration affect simulated flood extent and depth. It compares synthetic design-storm patterns with locally observed temporal distributions, showing why the same rainfall volume can produce different hazards. The work supports the use of rainfall patterns that reflect local storm behavior when flood maps are used for design or risk assessment.
