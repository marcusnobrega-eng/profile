Published in *Critical Reviews in Environmental Science and Technology*, this review connects low-impact development practices with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals. It organizes the environmental and social functions of these systems, identifies lessons from existing applications, and discusses barriers to broader implementation. The paper places urban stormwater management within a wider discussion of water security, climate adaptation, and sustainable cities.
